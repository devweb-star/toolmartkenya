Smart Fodder Storage for Kenyan Dairy Farmers

Secure your livestock's nutrition during the dry season with the Manual Hay Baler. Designed specifically for Kenyan dairy and zero-grazing farmers, this robust, human-powered compression machine allows you to tightly pack Napier grass, hay, desmodium, and maize stalks into dense, uniform bales.
