Step away from the physical strain of hand milking with the Milano Single Cow (1-Bucket) Milking Machine. Specifically designed for small-scale and beginner dairy farmers in Kenya managing 1 to 5 cows, this compact, affordable system brings modern hygiene and efficiency to your zero-grazing unit.
