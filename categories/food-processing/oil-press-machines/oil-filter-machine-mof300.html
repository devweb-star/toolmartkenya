Professional Purification for Premium Kenyan Cooking Oil

Transform cloudy, crude pressed oil into crystal-clear, premium cooking oil with the MOF300 Oil Filter Machine. After pressing sunflower, simsim (sesame), or groundnuts, the crude oil contains impurities, phospholipids, and moisture. This professional vacuum filtration system removes those impurities, ensuring your oil meets KEBS standards for retail packaging.

Designed for medium to large-scale agro-processors, the MOF300 operates continuously, filtering up to 300 liters per hour. Its stainless steel construction guarantees hygiene, while the vacuum technology prevents oil oxidation, preserving the natural aroma, color, and nutritional value of your cold-pressed oil.
