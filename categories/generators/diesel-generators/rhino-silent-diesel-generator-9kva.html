How the ATS (Automatic Transfer Switch) Works

  1. Normal Operation: Your home/office runs on KPLC mains power. The generator stays on standby.
  2. Power Failure Detected: ATS senses KPLC blackout within 3-5 seconds.
  3. Generator Auto-Starts: ATS signals the generator to start. Engine fires up automatically.
  4. Power Transfer: Within 10-15 seconds, ATS switches your load from KPLC to generator power.
  5. KPLC Returns: ATS detects mains power restoration, switches back to KPLC, and shuts down the generator automatically.

Result: You experience only a brief 10-15 second interruption. Your appliances stay safe and your life continues uninterrupted.
